# Sony research plan review

The supplied proposal source, bibliography, budget, and graphical abstract are retained in `materials-local/haptic-robot-learning/research-plans/`. They are proposal material rather than evidence of an award or completed research, so the website does not claim funding, acceptance, policy results, or autonomous performance.

## Research-line use

The proposal supports the long-term **Robot Imitation Learning** direction and frames a research question around haptic-guided human demonstration. Its planned pipeline comprises:

1. adapting the MR haptic actuator as a leader-side interface;
2. integrating leader–follower teleoperation, interaction sensing, and programmable kinesthetic feedback;
3. collecting synchronised motion, force, feedback, and task-outcome data;
4. training imitation-learning policies under matched conditions;
5. evaluating autonomous task performance, force, failure, robustness, generalisation, and data efficiency.

Precision insertion and rotational assembly are proposed experimental task classes. They must not be described as completed experiments.

## Current public evidence

The only current public material for this line is the pair of reviewed SO101 leader–follower teleoperation videos. They show the demonstration interface starting point, not a trained imitation-learning policy or learning result.
