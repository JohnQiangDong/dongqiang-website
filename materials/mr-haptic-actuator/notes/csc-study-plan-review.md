# CSC study plan review

The supplied 11-page study plan is retained in `materials-local/mr-haptic-actuator/research-plans/` because it contains a supervisor signature, collaboration details, future publication and patent intentions, and other application-specific material that should not be published wholesale.

## Research-line use

The document supports the long-term **MR Haptic Feedback** direction. Its central programme is broader than the current actuator project and includes:

- compact MRF interface design informed by magnetic, rheological, and tribological behaviour;
- adaptive force scaling, filtering, estimation, and force-tracking control;
- hardware-in-the-loop connection to biomechanical and tool–tissue interaction models;
- later validation in representative robotic interaction scenarios, with robot-assisted surgery as an important application direction.

## Publishing boundary

Only the existing actuator and preprint are described as current evidence. Hardware-in-the-loop integration, organ-phantom or ex vivo validation, patents, future papers, and clinical collaboration remain proposed work and must be labelled as research agenda rather than completed outcomes.
