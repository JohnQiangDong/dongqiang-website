slug: robot-imitation-learning
title: Robot Imitation Learning
order: 2
stage: early-stage
summary: Studying how human demonstrations, physical interaction sensing, and programmable kinesthetic feedback can support contact-rich robot learning.
question: Can haptic-guided demonstrations preserve interaction information that vision and motion trajectories alone may miss, and improve the quality and data efficiency of learned robot policies?
currentEvidence: This research line is newly started. Its public evidence is currently limited to two SO101 leader–follower teleoperation videos; no trained imitation-learning policy or learning result is claimed yet.
agenda:
  - Integrate leader–follower teleoperation with robot interaction sensing and real-time haptic mapping.
  - Collect synchronised robot states, actions, interaction forces, rendered feedback, and task outcomes.
  - Compare demonstrations collected under controlled feedback conditions for contact-rich manipulation.
  - Train and evaluate imitation-learning policies for task success, interaction force, robustness, generalisation, and data efficiency.
project: haptic-robot-learning
